please proofread this manuscript for final typesetting, including things what I listed below, as well as provide a document for everything you flagged for my review / approval

Text errors

  • Typos, especially the ones spellcheck approves: from/form, manger/manager, pubic/public, trail/trial, casual/causal
  • Wrong-word substitutions and doubled or dropped small words (the the, a missing not)
  • Punctuation: unclosed quotes and parens, comma/period placement relative to quote marks, spacing after periods
  • Dash and hyphen consistency — em vs. en vs. hyphen, and en dashes in number ranges
  • Straight quotes and apostrophes that escaped conversion to curly, and apostrophes curling the wrong way in ’90s or ’em
  • Capitalization and spelling consistency for names, places, and invented terms

Typesetting and layout

  • Bad line breaks and hyphenation, especially proper nouns and awkward splits
  • Widows and orphans, stacked hyphens at line ends, rivers of white space
  • Facing pages that don’t balance in depth
  • Running heads and folios: correct chapter titles, no folios on blank or display pages, numbering unbroken
  • Font, size, and leading consistency; heading hierarchy matching throughout
  • Indents, alignment, and spacing around block quotes, lists, and epigraphs

Cross-references and apparatus

  • Table of contents titles and page numbers matching the actual pages
  • Footnote or endnote numbering unbroken, notes on the page they belong to, no orphaned callouts
  • Internal references (“see chapter 4,” “the table below”) pointing to the right place after pagination shifted
  • Figure and table numbers, captions, and credits correct and matching the text mentions
  • Index entries hitting the right pages, if the index has been set

Hyperlink footnotes that don’t make sense or you think shouldn’t be there for the purpose of printing
